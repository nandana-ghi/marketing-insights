# Marketing Campaign Insights

## Summary
Overall marketing performance was heavily driven by high-efficiency channels, with Instagram and Email generating the bulk of conversions at remarkably low acquisition costs. In contrast, top-of-funnel and Facebook efforts lagged, particularly YouTube which drove only 5 conversions at a costly $60.00 CPA.

## Top Insight
Instagram's 'Festive Offer' generated the highest overall volume (85 conversions) while maintaining an exceptional $3.65 cost per conversion and an 8.95% conversion rate.

## Campaigns Needing Attention
- Brand Awareness
- Back to School
